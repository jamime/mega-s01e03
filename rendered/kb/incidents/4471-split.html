How to tell them apart

  • Parser. The report came by app or email, or through a booking-v2 form, from a tenant that writes the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April becomes 4 July.
  • Hint. The report came through the form of a booking-v3 tenant. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY, people type month first, and the form reads the entry the tenant’s way. The text looks the same as a parser report, so only the channel and the tenant’s form separate them. A parser fix does not change this form.
  • One-day shift. The date moves by one day: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. No swap of day and month does that. One report has the same meeting on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, so look at time zones first. That cause is not confirmed.

Do not count from these

  • The intake tag. It follows the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. Every report tagged hint came from an en-US browser, wherever the tenant is and whichever form it runs, so the tag misses hint reports and marks parser reports as hint. The desk’s table below shows both errors.
  • The raw export. Its pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Count by id.
  • Subject lines. Decision 0001 treats a matching subject as the same issue. In these reports, the same subject appears under different problems, and one problem has many subjects.
